HOD Assignment Rules (Quick View)

Expertise Match: Instructor must align with course field.
Experience: Senior courses = senior instructors.
Workload Limit: Max 9–12 credit hours/instructor.
Course Difficulty: Complex topics = capable hands.
Performance: Based on past results & feedback.
Backlogs: Prefer same teacher for repeats.
Practical Courses: Assign to hands-on experts.
Preferences: Consider instructor's interest if possible.
Extra Duties: Reduce load for admin-heavy staff.
Rotation: Alternate instructors every cycle.
Mid-Semester Exit: HOD can revoke course anytime.
Rule Example: “Instructor with 3+ years in Software Engineering and under 6 credit hours → assigned CSC-502.”
